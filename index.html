Mahesh's YouTube channel tools

This page is the home of a few small, self-hosted programs that publish videos to YouTube channels run by Mahesh Srivastava:

  • Pariksha Saathi uploader: daily exam-preparation videos.
  • Siya's Spotlight uploader: sung nursery rhymes for toddlers.
  • Shiv Bhajan Uploader: a daily original Hindi Shiv bhajan lyric video.

Each program runs on the owner's own computer, creates a video locally and uploads it to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API. They are not offered as a service to anyone else and have no users other than the channel owner.

What the Google sign-in is for

When the owner signs in with Google, the programs ask for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the owner's channel and video list (youtube.readonly) so they can confirm an upload succeeded. Nothing else is requested or read.
