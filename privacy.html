Privacy policy

Applies to the Pariksha Saathi uploader, the Siya's Spotlight uploader and the Shiv Bhajan Uploader (together, "the programs"). Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who uses the programs

The programs are private tools run by their owner on the owner's own computer to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. They are not distributed to, or operated for, any other person. The only Google account that signs in to them is the owner's.

What data the programs access

Through the YouTube Data API, with the owner's consent given at sign-in, the programs:

  • upload video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails created by the programs to the owner's channel (scope youtube.upload);
  • read the owner's channel name and the list and status of the owner's own videos, to confirm uploads and avoid duplicates (scope youtube.readonly).

The programs do not access viewers' data, comments, subscriber lists, analytics, contacts, email or any Google data other than the owner's own channel and videos.

How data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues at sign-in are stored only in a file on the owner's computer, readable by the owner's user account alone, and are used solely to call the YouTube Data API on the owner's behalf. No data obtained from Google is sent to any third party, sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling. Video files and metadata are generated and kept locally.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The programs' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time by removing the app from Google account permissions or by deleting the local token file; the programs then stop working until a new sign-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the GitHub repository for this site.
